### Human priority and bounded capacity

A pinned human/legacy owner blocks guarded issuance and guarded first consumption even with zero Viewers after grace and ticket TTL. Return HTTP 409 `human-owner-retained` at issue; socket rejection at consume. Only no owner or a prior guarded owner may be replaced by a newly guarded ticket under all remaining gates. Do not clear owner, restart the server, consume proof or manufacture a human session to make qualification possible. A necessary combined Signal code release creates a fresh runtime normally; a human who arrives afterward makes unattended qualification yield. This preserves ordinary human resume eligibility and avoids introducing dual owners or a special human resume takeover.

`sequence` remains the human-intent high-water fence. Human issue increments it; guarded issue captures it without incrementing. Guard issuance rejects `human-intent-pending` if any ticket is unexpired, unconsumed and human, including older human tickets whose consume would already be stale. This conservative rule lasts only their existing TTL. A later successful human issue increments the fence and invalidates all pending guards permanently, including after the human ticket expires. Human acceptance additionally changes owner and Viewer epoch, so a human that connects and leaves before guarded consume still wins.

To prevent guards occupying all capacity from blocking a human Start, successful human issuance may remove **only unconsumed guarded tickets**, after validating the human request and computing available capacity. Compute effective live capacity excluding those revocable guarded records; if still full, reject 429 without modifying a record or fence. If it fits, prune expired records, remove unconsumed guards, advance sequence and insert the human ticket atomically. Never evict consumed records or live human tickets. Guarded issuance uses total live capacity and cannot remove another live record. This is smaller than a reserved-slot quota and does not reduce the documented capacity.

All guarded rejection paths preserve owner, pristine compatibility state, sequence, proof records/epoch, Viewer map, lease and Host events. They do not consume, remove or rewrite a live guarded ticket. Expired records may be pruned only on a successful issue or by the existing ordinary cleanup path; guard validation itself is read-only on rejection. Use one captured `now()` per operation. `capacity` is bounded, so live/pending scans remain at most 256 records.
